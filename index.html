The research

Based on Article 3 of Lidiane Souza's doctoral thesis, The hierarchy of the dimensions of cognitive flexibility: importance assigned by project team professionals (Souza & Martins, article in progress). The study surveyed 210 professionals who work in project teams with the PAPRIKA pairwise method (1000minds). The seven profiles come from an exploratory cluster analysis of modest quality (mean silhouette 0.256): read them as tendencies, not fixed types.
